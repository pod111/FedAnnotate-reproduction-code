Figure output directory.

Figure 4 of the paper is regenerated by:
    python -m fedannotate.run_tradeoff     # writes _tradeoff.json
    python -m fedannotate.make_fig4        # writes fig4_chinese_eps_tradeoff.{png,jpeg,tiff}

Files are written at 600 dpi. Figures 1-3 of the paper are schematic diagrams
and the CoNLL-2003 prototype plot; they are produced by the authors' drawing
toolchain and are not regenerated here.
